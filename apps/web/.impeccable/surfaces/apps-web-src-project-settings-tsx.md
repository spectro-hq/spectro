## THESIS

An organization administrator can invite a colleague and understand their authority without leaving the familiar Settings workspace.

## OWN-WORLD

Extend Spectro's incumbent quiet grey/violet Console: native typography, restrained one-border panels, compact controls, clear focus, no invented avatars or ornamental metrics. Preserve existing Project settings, shell, logo and navigation.

## STORY

Settings separates project-scoped browser keys from organization-wide Members. The directory explains roles. Administrators explicitly create and copy a one-time invitation link; recipients sign in with a verified matching email, review the organization and role, and explicitly join. Errors explain recovery; no automatic enrollment or email delivery claim.

## FIRST-VIEWPORT

The Settings section switch, organization heading, member directory and Invite member action should be immediately findable. Invitations sit below the directory; a freshly created link gets a temporary save/copy area with expiry and manual delivery instructions.

## FORM

Precise local extension of the accepted Settings composition, not a new visual-world tournament. Project / Members links preserve scope in the URL. Responsive rows stack on narrow screens; read-only members retain readable role descriptions. The invite acceptance form reuses the established workspace gate. Loading, empty, error, pending, expired, accepted and revoked states are explicit. Confirmation protects destructive and ownership changes. No new raster assets.
